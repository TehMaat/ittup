# Code of Conduct

ITTup adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and experience, discuss ideas
and not people. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not accepted.

The maintainers may remove comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this code, and may ban
temporarily or permanently anyone who doesn't respect it.

To report a behavior, write privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/TehMaat/ittup/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only them) or to the maintainers on GitHub. Every report is reviewed confidentially.
